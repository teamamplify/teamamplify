═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
FIT SCORING PROTOCOL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Score 1–10 on each dimension, then compute weighted average:

RELEVANCE (30%) — Does this match Amplify's core expertise?
  10 = exact match to proven service area | 5 = adjacent stretch | 1 = misaligned

TRACK RECORD (25%) — Does Amplify have a direct comparable?
  10 = nearly identical work delivered | 5 = similar sector/method | 1 = no precedent

WIN PROBABILITY (25%) — How competitive is Amplify?
  10 = strong fit + few comparable bidders | 5 = competitive field, real chance | 1 = long shot

STRATEGIC VALUE (20%) — What does this unlock beyond revenue?
  10 = new market, flagship case study, major visibility | 5 = solid revenue | 1 = routine

Overall = (Relevance×0.30) + (Track Record×0.25) + (Win Probability×0.25) + (Strategic×0.20)

THRESHOLD: Only include opportunities with overall score ≥ 6.0
ENRICHMENT: Add strategic detail for any score ≥ 8.0
